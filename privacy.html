Privacy Policy

FiveStarMoments · Effective November 18, 2026

FiveStarMoments is a personal tool run by one individual (the "operator"). It finds highlight moments in public Twitch gaming streams, edits them into short vertical videos, and publishes them to the operator's own TikTok and YouTube accounts. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

1. Who uses the app

Only the operator signs in to FiveStarMoments. The app is not offered to the public, and no other person can create an account or connect their TikTok account to it.

2. Data we receive from TikTok

When the operator connects their TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives:

  • Basic account info (user.info.basic): the account's open ID, display name and avatar. This is used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens: used only to upload videos to that account through the Content Posting API (video.publish), and to check the posting options TikTok allows for it.

The app does not read the operator's followers, messages, likes, viewing history, or other users' data.

3. Data from public streams

To find highlights, the app processes publicly broadcast Twitch streams: their video, their audio (turned into a transcript), and public chat messages, which are used only to measure how strongly the audience reacted. Chat messages are kept briefly for this purpose and are never published, sold, or used to identify people.

4. How data is stored

All data is stored on a private server controlled by the operator. TikTok tokens are kept in a file that only the operator can access, and they are deleted when the TikTok account is disconnected.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent data, and we do not share it with advertisers. Data is sent only to the services needed to run the app: TikTok and YouTube (to publish videos), and an AI provider (Anthropic) that receives stream transcripts and chat excerpts to judge which moments are worth clipping.

6. Your choices

  • The operator can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Apps and services.
  • Streamers who want their content excluded or a video removed can contact us (see below). We will remove it and stop clipping their channel.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Questions or removal requests: open a request on GitHub
